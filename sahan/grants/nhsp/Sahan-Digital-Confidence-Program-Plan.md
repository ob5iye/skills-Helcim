## 3. Course Catalog — 24-Module Curriculum

Six learning phases, each spans 4 weekends. Every module = one weekend (Sat instruction + Sun reinforcement).

### Phase 1 — Foundations (Weekends 1–4)
| # | Module | Saturday outcome | Sunday reinforcement |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Device basics | Power on/off, charge, touchscreen, accessibility settings (font size, voice) | Hands-on practice with peer-mentor |
| M2 | Typing & navigation | Touch keyboard in English + community language; gestures | Typing practice cards |
| M3 | Internet browsing | Open browser, type URL, search, bookmark | Find 3 useful websites |
| M4 | Account setup | Create Apple ID or Google account; manage password | Set up cohort group account safety |

### Phase 2 — Communication (Weekends 5–8)
| # | Module | Saturday outcome | Sunday reinforcement |
|---|---|---|---|
| M5 | WhatsApp basics | Send messages, voice notes; join family group | Family Hour: message a relative live |
| M6 | Video calls | FaceTime / WhatsApp video / Zoom | Family Hour: video call overseas |
| M7 | Email | Read, reply, attach, recognize spam | Send a message to instructor |
| M8 | Photos & sharing | Take, view, share photos with family | Family photo exchange |

### Phase 3 — Online Safety & Fraud Awareness (Weekends 9–12) — **core NHSP "elder abuse" content**
| # | Module | Saturday outcome | Sunday reinforcement |
|---|---|---|---|
| M9 | Recognizing phishing | Spot fake emails, SMS, CRA scams | Family Hour: review real scam examples |
| M10 | CRA & grandparent scams | **Calgary Police Service Elder Abuse Response Team guest session** | Role-play scam phone calls |
| M11 | Romance & giveaway scams | Recognize emotional manipulation tactics | Multilingual scam-prevention takeaways |
| M12 | Passwords, 2FA, digital hygiene | Strong passwords; 2-factor authentication; safe browsing | Family Hour: secure a family member's account |

### Phase 4 — Government Services (Weekends 13–16)
| # | Module | Saturday outcome | Sunday reinforcement |
|---|---|---|---|
| M13 | My Service Canada | Create account; navigate dashboard | Practice login independently |
| M14 | OAS & GIS applications | Walk through application screens | Each elder verifies own status |
| M15 | My CRA Account | Access notice of assessment, tax slips | Find a real document from CRA |
| M16 | IRCC online | Check PR status, citizenship application progress | Update contact info online |

### Phase 5 — Healthcare & Banking (Weekends 17–20)
| # | Module | Saturday outcome | Sunday reinforcement |
|---|---|---|---|
| M17 | MyAlbertaHealth | Register, view records | Locate vaccine record |
| M18 | Telehealth & online booking | Book a real appointment | Family Hour: book a relative's appointment |
| M19 | Online banking | Set up app, view balance | Practice login + 2FA |
| M20 | e-Transfer & bill pay | Send Interac e-Transfer; pay a bill | Send small e-Transfer to instructor (refunded) |

### Phase 6 — Application & Tech Circle Launch (Weekends 21–24)
| # | Module | Saturday outcome | Sunday reinforcement |
|---|---|---|---|
| M21 | "Apply It" Day | Each elder completes one real-life online task they choose | Document the win |
| M22 | Family Digital Safety workshop | Whole household session — bring 1 family member | Multilingual family fraud guide takeaway |
| M23 | Tech Circle peer leadership training | Mentors learn to run drop-in sessions | First peer-led Tech Circle hour |
| M24 | Graduation + Digital Inclusion Day | Open community event; certificate ceremony | Tech Circle becomes monthly |

### Sustainment Phase (Months 7–9, after Weekend 24)
- Monthly Senior Tech Circle continues, led by trained peer-mentors
- Equipment remains with Sahan for ongoing community use
- Two reunion events for cohort
- Final evaluation, ESDC report

## 9. Should You Add Elder Names to the Application?

**Short answer: yes, but strategically — do not paste 20 names into form fields.**

| Action | Do it? | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Paste all 20 names into a form text field | **No** | Privacy risk; reviewers don't read individual names; clutters the form |
| Mention "20 elders are already named and committed to Cohort 1" in the Project Summary and Community Need narratives | **Yes** | Already in your drafts — strong evidence of demand |
| Attach a "Participant Intent Declaration" with 20 signatures as a supporting document | **YES — high priority** | Single PDF: brief statement ("We, the undersigned, commit to participating in this program") + 20 lines for printed name + signature |
| Name the 4 Senior Leadership Committee members in the planning/design section | **Yes** | List them by full name in the "describe their role" field — shows real people leading |
| Submit list of names as "participant list" | **No** | NHSP doesn't require this and surfaces unnecessary personal data |

**Bottom line:** Two named places (the 4 SLC members in narrative + 20 signatures on the Intent Declaration attachment). That's the optimal blend of demonstrating real commitment without exposing participants' identities into the GCOS form fields.

## 10. Application Success Factors — What Actually Wins NHSP $25K Awards

Eight things to lock in before submitting. Treat as a non-negotiable checklist.

1. **Lower-than-cap ask.** $25K vs. $50K cap signals discipline → reviewers prefer it.
2. **High per-elder cost in healthy range.** $1,250/elder is on the high side, which signals quality, not waste, because we have substantive in-kind to balance it.
3. **Named, committed participants.** The 20-elder Intent Declaration is your strongest evidence.
4. **Senior-led design, on paper.** SLC must be named, with explicit role + paid honoraria.
5. **Concrete partnerships.** Three letters minimum: mosque, library/settlement agency, Calgary Police.
6. **Two priority objectives + capital justification.** Mentoring + Social Inclusion + Capital Assistance. National priority = Celebrating Diversity + Preventing Senior Abuse.
7. **Clean budget with no math errors.** Totals must reconcile across Budget screen and Anticipated Sources of Contribution screen. Have a second person check.
8. **Sustainability beyond the funded year.** Tech Circle continues monthly, peer-mentors trained, equipment retained — all stated explicitly.
