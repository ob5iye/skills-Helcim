# NHSP Application — Project Details Drafts
**Project:** Digital Confidence for Older Newcomers
**Applicant:** SAHAN Heritage Foundation
**Start:** January 16, 2027 | **End:** January 15, 2028

---

## 1. Project Summary (500 words or less)

African newcomer seniors in Calgary face compounded barriers as they settle in Canada: limited English, social isolation, and growing digital exclusion. Essential services — applying for Old Age Security and the Guaranteed Income Supplement, booking healthcare appointments, communicating with the Canada Revenue Agency, banking, and connecting with family overseas — are now overwhelmingly online. Without digital skills and confidence, seniors in our community cannot independently access the supports they have earned, navigate Canadian systems, or stay connected to their grandchildren's school lives. Sahan Heritage Foundation works closely with this community and consistently hears from elders that "not knowing the computer" is a daily source of stress and dependency.

Digital Confidence for Older Newcomers is a 12-month senior-led project that will equip 80 African newcomer seniors (age 55+) in Calgary with the digital skills, confidence, and peer-support networks they need to participate fully in Canadian life. The project will be designed and delivered by a Senior Leadership Committee of 8 elders who will set curriculum priorities, co-facilitate sessions, and mentor newer participants. Activities will be hosted at familiar community spaces — community centres, partner mosques, and Calgary Public Library branches — in Northeast Calgary, where the majority of our seniors live.

Anticipated results: (1) 80 newcomer seniors complete a 10-week digital literacy curriculum covering device basics, online safety, video calling, online government services, telehealth, and online banking; (2) 8 senior peer-mentors are trained and lead small-group sessions; (3) seniors report reduced isolation and increased confidence accessing services independently (measured pre/post); (4) at least 30 seniors successfully complete an online task that matters to them — a CRA login, an OAS application, a doctor's appointment booking, or a video call with family abroad; (5) a sustainable monthly Senior Tech Circle continues beyond the funded year.

Target group: African newcomer seniors aged 55+ living in Calgary, with priority for those who arrived in Canada within the past 10 years, women, and seniors living alone. We expect strong representation from Somali, Sudanese, Eritrean, Ethiopian, and Oromo communities, reflecting Sahan's existing community ties.

This is a new project for Sahan Heritage Foundation. We have run cultural celebrations, family recreation, and community gatherings since our founding, and through that work we have heard directly from seniors that digital literacy is their most pressing unmet need. This NHSP project formalizes a seniors-specific stream of our work, builds our capacity to serve older adults, and creates an explicit leadership role for elders within our organization — strengthening intergenerational connection and ensuring seniors shape the programs that serve them.

## 2. Project Activities and Timelines

**Phase 1 — Setup and Senior Leadership Committee (Months 1–2: January–February 2027)**
- Recruit and onboard 8 senior peer-mentors as the Senior Leadership Committee.
- Co-design the 10-week curriculum with the Committee, Calgary Public Library's digital literacy team, and partner settlement agencies.
- Procure 20 tablets and accessibility accessories (large-print keyboards, headphones, screen magnifiers).
- Secure venue partnerships (Genesis Centre, partner mosques, Calgary Public Library branches in Northeast Calgary).
- Recruit Cohort 1 (40 seniors) through Sahan's community channels and partner referrals.

**Phase 2 — Cohort 1 delivery (Months 3–5: March–May 2027)**
- Deliver the 10-week digital literacy curriculum to Cohort 1.
- Weekly 2-hour sessions, small-group format (5–6 seniors per peer-mentor).
- Topics: device basics, internet safety and fraud awareness, video calls, MyCRA, MyAlbertaHealth, online banking, accessing OAS/GIS, navigating IRCC online services.
- Pre/post confidence and skills assessments.

**Phase 3 — Cohort 2 delivery and mid-project evaluation (Months 6–8: June–August 2027)**
- Deliver the 10-week curriculum to Cohort 2 (40 seniors).
- Mid-project participant focus groups; curriculum refinements informed by Cohort 1 feedback.
- Senior Tech Circle (monthly, peer-led drop-in) launches.

**Phase 4 — Tech Circle expansion and outreach events (Months 9–10: September–October 2027)**
- Senior Tech Circle continues monthly.
- Two community "Digital Inclusion Days" — open events for elders not yet enrolled, in partnership with local mosques and seniors-serving organizations.
- Alumni mentorship begins: graduates support new learners.

**Phase 5 — Evaluation, sustainability, and reporting (Months 11–12: November 2027–January 2028)**
- Final participant outcome surveys and interviews.
- Sustainability plan for the ongoing Tech Circle.
- Final narrative and financial report to ESDC.
- Knowledge-sharing event with partner organizations to share what worked.

## 3. How the project meets NHSP mandatory objectives and priorities (500 words or less)

This project meets all five NHSP mandatory objectives.

**(1) Promote volunteerism among seniors and other generations.** The Senior Leadership Committee — 8 elders trained as peer-mentors — is the volunteer engine of the project. Seniors design and deliver, not just receive. We will also engage 6 youth volunteers from Sahan's existing programs as tech-support assistants, building genuine intergenerational volunteer relationships and giving young people a structured way to give back to their elders.

**(2) Engage seniors in the community through mentoring of others.** The peer-mentor model is the project's core. Senior mentors lead small groups; alumni graduates become mentors for subsequent cohorts. By month 12 we project at least 16 seniors will have served as active mentors, with a mentor pipeline that continues after the funded year.

**(3) Expand awareness of elder abuse, including financial abuse.** Online safety and fraud prevention is a dedicated module of the curriculum (week 4). Seniors learn to recognize phishing, romance scams, CRA-impersonation calls, and grandparent scams — frauds that disproportionately target newcomer elders who are unfamiliar with Canadian institutions. We will partner with the Calgary Police Service's Elder Abuse Response Team for one guest session per cohort and distribute multilingual fraud-prevention resources.

**(4) Support social participation and inclusion of seniors.** The project directly combats senior isolation by creating sustained in-person learning communities and a monthly Tech Circle that continues beyond the funded year. Sessions are held in trusted, culturally familiar spaces and delivered in plain English supported by community-language peer assistance (Somali, Arabic, Amharic, Tigrinya, Oromo). Seniors gain not only skills but a regular community of peers — a durable anti-isolation outcome.

**(5) Provide capital assistance for new and existing community projects for seniors.** The tablets, accessibility equipment, and curriculum materials procured through this project remain with Sahan and the Senior Tech Circle after the funded year, providing ongoing capital for senior-led programming and a permanent loaner library for seniors who cannot afford their own devices.

**Priorities — vulnerable populations.** The project explicitly targets newcomer seniors (an NHSP-recognized priority population), racialized seniors, and seniors at risk of social isolation. Within each cohort we will prioritize women, seniors living alone, and elders who arrived in Canada within the past 10 years. Outreach materials will be available in English, Somali, Arabic, Amharic, Tigrinya, and Oromo, delivered through Sahan's existing trusted community channels — including faith leaders, women's circles, and community elders — ensuring we reach seniors who would not be reached by mainstream English-language outreach.
