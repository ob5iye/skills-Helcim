## Description of the Initiative

The KULAN Senior Sharing and Caring Project is a community-based initiative designed to support Black and newcomer older adults in Edmonton to stay active, healthy, and socially connected. The project focuses on improving the sense of inclusion and quality of life of seniors by creating age-friendly environments and providing opportunities for learning, engagement, and community connection.

The initiative addresses challenges faced by older adults, including social isolation, language barriers, limited access to information, and the growing generational gap between seniors and youth. Through intergenerational activities, the project creates opportunities for seniors and youth to interact, share stories, and learn from one another, increasing understanding of cultural values, historical experiences, and ageism.

Planned activities include meetings, workshops, and training sessions to better understand local community needs related to aging; culturally appropriate information sharing through Somali-language flyers; nutrition and cooking classes focused on healthy food preparation for seniors; community meals to reach isolated older adults; safety and fall prevention workshops; and community events that bring together different cultural groups to share best practices around aging. The project also supports seniors with low literacy, limited English proficiency, and those at risk of health and social isolation by providing accessible information on elder abuse prevention, fraud awareness, and available supports.

Through these activities, the project aims to strengthen community connections, increase volunteer engagement, and support older adults in remaining socially engaged, informed, and supported within their community.

## Number of Participants
Approximately 60–70 participants

## Funding Requested
$45,000

## Use of Funds
Funds will be used to support the delivery of the KULAN Senior Sharing and Caring Project activities, including community meetings, workshops, classes, and events designed to support Black and newcomer older adults in Edmonton. This includes costs associated with organizing and facilitating meetings, workshops, and training sessions focused on community needs related to aging, safety, fall prevention, and elder abuse awareness.

Funding will also support the development and distribution of culturally appropriate information materials, including Somali-language flyers, to ensure seniors with limited English proficiency can access relevant information and services. Additional costs will support food preparation, nutrition, and cooking classes for seniors, as well as community meals that provide opportunities for social connection and engagement, particularly for isolated older adults.

Funds will further support intergenerational and community engagement activities that bring together seniors, youth, volunteers, and community members to share knowledge, build relationships, and address issues such as ageism, social isolation, and community safety. Resources will also be used to support planning, coordination, monitoring, and evaluation of project activities in collaboration with seniors, community leaders, and volunteers.

## Reporting
KULAN will report back to the Sienna For Seniors Foundation by documenting how grant funds were used to support the delivery of the Senior Sharing and Caring Project activities, including meetings, workshops, classes, information materials, and community events. Financial reporting will reflect expenditures related to project delivery, coordination, and implementation as outlined in the approved initiative plan.

The impact of the initiative on participating seniors will be reported through information gathered during project activities, including feedback from seniors, community members, volunteers, and community leaders involved in the planning, monitoring, and evaluation process. Reporting will highlight participation in workshops, classes, and community meals, as well as seniors' experiences related to increased social connection, access to information, and engagement in community activities. Observations from meetings, workshops, and intergenerational activities will be used to describe how the project supported inclusion, learning, safety awareness, and quality of life for participating seniors.

## About the Organization

### Organizational Expertise and Track Record
The Horn Youth Services Foundation (KULAN) has several years of experience delivering community-based, culturally responsive programs that support Black and newcomer communities in Edmonton, including seniors, youth, families, and children. KULAN provides social services such as advice, counselling, interpreting, integration support, and information related to housing, education, employment, and community resources for members of the Black community, including older adults.

KULAN has demonstrated experience engaging seniors through community meetings, workshops, information sessions, and culturally appropriate outreach, with a focus on reducing isolation, increasing access to information, and strengthening community connections. The organization works closely with community leaders, parents, seniors, and volunteers to design and deliver programming that reflects community priorities and lived experience.

The organization has a proven track record of managing community-based initiatives involving planning, coordination, monitoring, and evaluation, with active involvement from community members throughout the process. KULAN's programs are supported by an engaged Board of Directors and delivered by staff and volunteers with strong ties to the community. This experience positions KULAN to effectively manage and deliver initiatives that promote healthy aging, social inclusion, and intergenerational connection among Black and newcomer seniors in Edmonton.

## Additional Information

### Additional Comments
The KULAN Senior Sharing and Caring Project is grounded in community-identified needs and has been shaped through ongoing engagement with seniors, community leaders, parents, and volunteers. The initiative reflects lived experiences within Black and newcomer communities in Edmonton and is designed to be culturally responsive, accessible, and inclusive of seniors with limited English proficiency, low literacy, and those at risk of social or health isolation.

This application reflects KULAN's commitment to working collaboratively with community members to strengthen social connection, intergenerational understanding, and quality of life for older adults in Edmonton. Support from the Sienna For Seniors Foundation would help advance these community-led efforts to ensure seniors feel valued, included, and supported within their community.
