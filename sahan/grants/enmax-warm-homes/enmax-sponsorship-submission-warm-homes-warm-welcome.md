## 2. The Initiative (as proposed)

Three free evening sessions (2 hours each), January–February 2027, at one donated Calgary
venue (Somali Canadian Society of Calgary space — in-kind letter exists), timed to newcomer
families' first Canadian winter:

1. **"Understanding Your First Utility Bill"** — what you're paying for, retailer vs.
   Regulated Rate, reading a real sample bill (ucahelps.alberta.ca materials)
2. **"Winter-Proofing Your Home"** — heating safely, carbon monoxide detectors, frozen
   pipes, low-cost draft-proofing, thermostat habits
3. **"Where to Get Help"** — 211 Alberta, ENMAX payment support, affordability programs + Q&A

Each session includes a hot community dinner; every family takes home translated utility
guides and a winter efficiency kit. Delivered by trained Sahan volunteer ambassadors; open
invitation extended to an ENMAX employee speaker.

**Measurement:** attendance tracked at each session + short pre/post knowledge check;
short report with photos promised to ENMAX after the series.

## 5. Application — Section 3: Opportunity Information

**Program start date:** January 15, 2027
**Expected reach:** 100–250

**Summary of initiative (as submitted — final human-voice version):**

> Facing your first Canadian winter is tough, especially when trying to understand an Alberta utility bill and safely heat a home. That is why we created Warm Homes, Warm Welcome. We are hosting three free evening sessions in Calgary this January and February 2027 to help new families stay warm and save money.
>
> To make it easy for busy parents to attend, we serve a hot community dinner while our trained Sahan volunteers share practical energy advice. We also welcome an ENMAX employee to come speak. Families learn how to read their bills, choose an energy provider, and access support programs like 211 and ENMAX payment help. We also cover crucial winter heating and carbon monoxide safety.
>
> Every family leaves with translated utility guides and a free winter efficiency kit. With weather stripping, window film, and LED bulbs, they can make their homes warmer that exact same night. For many newcomers, this is their first time dealing with a freezing winter. This program gives them the knowledge and supplies they need to feel secure and welcome in Calgary.

**What are you requesting from ENMAX (as submitted):**

> We are asking for a $1,500 community sponsorship. It covers three simple things: hot community dinners at all three sessions ($750), translated utility guides families can keep ($400), and winter efficiency kits for 30 newcomer households ($300). Our venue space is donated, so every dollar goes straight to the families.
>
> We would also love to welcome an ENMAX employee as a guest speaker at one of the sessions. Hearing directly from an energy expert would mean a lot to parents who are navigating bills and heating for the very first time.
>
> In return, we will proudly recognize ENMAX on our printed guides and at every session. After the series wraps up, we will send you a short report with photos and attendance numbers so you can see exactly what your support made possible.

**Other details to help with the decision (as submitted):**

> ENMAX has already shown that this kind of education works. Your Energy Efficient Futures workshops with Green Calgary help people understand their bills and save energy at home. We want to bring that same practical learning to the families who need it most and are hardest to reach — newcomers who have just arrived and are facing their very first winter.
>
> We may be small, but we are proven. Sahan is a registered Alberta society that has raised over $20,000 through five community events, and we already hold a $5,000 City of Calgary microgrant. We track attendance at every session and use a quick before-and-after quiz, so we will know exactly how much these families learned.

**Anything else we want them to know (as submitted):**

> This is Sahan's first corporate sponsorship request, and we kept it small on purpose. A modest, concrete ask is something a volunteer-run group like ours can deliver well and report on honestly.
>
> If $1,500 is more than is available right now, any partial amount would still put hot meals and warm homes within reach for these families. Even an ENMAX speaker at a single session would be a gift.
>
> Thank you for considering a small grassroots organization. With your support, thirty newcomer families will face their first Calgary winter feeling safe, prepared, and genuinely welcomed.

## 6. Funder Context (why this application was built this way)

- **ENMAX priority pillars:** Essential Needs (warm meals for vulnerable families); Energy
  Safety, Education & Efficiency; Enriching Communities; Scholarships. This initiative
  targets the first two.
- **36% of ENMAX's community investment budget** supports energy affordability/essential needs.
- **Precedent cited:** Green Calgary's "Energy Efficient Futures" workshops (ENMAX-funded,
  community-delivered utility literacy) — this initiative brings the same model to newcomer
  families, an audience those programs don't reach.
- **ENMAX exclusions (confirmed eligible):** no religious-purpose programs, political,
  lotteries, individuals — Sahan qualifies as a secular registered not-for-profit society.
- **Recognition offered:** logo/name on printed guides, verbal recognition at each session,
  post-series report with photos.
- **Partial-yes option included deliberately:** "any partial amount" fallback makes approval
  easier for a first-time small-org ask.
