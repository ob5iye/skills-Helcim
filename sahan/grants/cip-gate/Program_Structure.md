# Rise Together: Pathways for Newcomer Women

A culturally grounded employment readiness, English language, and community-belonging program for newcomer women — many of whom are single mothers facing language, employment, and childcare barriers. Designed to remove the practical obstacles that prevent women from accessing existing services, and to build cross-community connection through a partnership component.

---

## Target Population

- Newcomer women in Edmonton, primarily East African and Black communities
- Includes women who do not speak English fluently
- Includes single mothers who are unemployed or under-employed
- Includes women who have been in Canada for years but have never entered the Canadian workforce (e.g., raised children at home, were caregivers)

---

## Program Length & Cohort Size

- **15 women per cohort**
- **12 weeks per cohort**
- **1 cohort delivered through this funded cycle**
- Total reach: 15 women + approximately 15–25 of their children + 4 mentors

---

## Weekly Schedule

The program runs **one Saturday per week** for 12 consecutive Saturdays.

| Time | Activity | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 9:45 – 10:00 am | Arrival, sign-in, children handed to babysitters | 15 min |
| 10:00 am – 1:00 pm | **Session 1 — Job Readiness Workshop** | 3 hours |
| 1:00 – 2:00 pm | **Hot lunch** (women and children eat together) | 1 hour |
| 2:00 – 4:00 pm | **Session 2 — Conversational English + Mentor Time** | 2 hours |
| 4:00 – 4:15 pm | Pickup, weekly handout, debrief | 15 min |

**Contact hours per participant:** 5 hours per Saturday × 12 weeks = **60 hours total per cohort**

---

## Session Content

### Session 1 (10am – 1pm) — Job Readiness Workshop

Rotating weekly themes building toward employment outcomes:

- **Weeks 1–2:** Self-assessment, identifying transferable skills, Canadian workplace introduction
- **Weeks 3–4:** Resume building using ESL-adapted templates
- **Weeks 5–6:** Cover letters, online job applications, job board navigation
- **Weeks 7–8:** Interview preparation, mock interviews with feedback
- **Weeks 9–10:** Workplace communication — phone calls, professional email, workplace norms
- **Weeks 11–12:** Networking, mentor matching, individual next-steps planning

### Session 2 (2pm – 4pm) — Conversational English + Mentorship

- **First hour:** ESL focused on workplace vocabulary tied to that week's morning topic (e.g., interview language during interview-prep weeks)
- **Second hour:** Small-group mentor circles — participants split into groups of 3–4 with a mentor for one-on-one and group discussion

## Children's Room (Where the Kids Will Be)

A separate room at the venue is designated as the children's room for the entire program day (9:45 am – 4:15 pm).

- **Staffed by 2 registered/certified babysitters** present the full day
- **Capacity:** up to 20 children, mixed ages from infant to ~age 10
- **Setup:** play mats, age-grouped toy stations, quiet area for naps, snack/lunch table
- **Children eat lunch with their mothers** at 1pm so families have meal time together
- **Snacks** provided mid-morning and mid-afternoon
- **Sign-in/out logs** for every child every Saturday (safety and insurance requirement)
- **Halal-only food** for children's snacks and meals, with vegetarian option always available

## Bridges Event (Cross-Community Partnership)

**What it is:** During Week 8 of the cohort, the standard Session 2 is replaced with a joint session that brings together the participants and a small group of women (approximately 5–8) from a partner organization in the Jewish community for a shared 2-hour cooking, storytelling, and skill-share circle.

**Why it matters:**
- Builds tolerance, anti-racism, and social inclusion across communities
- Gives participants direct exposure to working women from another community (potential mentors and professional contacts)
- Demonstrates to funders a real cross-cultural partnership, not a token one
- Counts toward "social inclusion" and "community wellbeing" outcomes

**Logistics:** Same venue, same childcare available, lunch extended into the afternoon to include all participants from both groups.

## Program Outcomes (What Will Be Measured)

- Number of women completing the 12-week cohort (target: 13 of 15)
- Number of women with a finalized resume by program end (target: 15 of 15)
- Number of women completing at least one mock interview (target: 13 of 15)
- Self-reported confidence in workplace English (pre/post survey)
- Number of women applying to at least one job by program end (target: 10 of 15)
- Number of mentor relationships established (target: 15)
- Number of children safely cared for across the cohort (target: 15–25)
- Attendance rate across all 12 Saturdays (target: ≥75% per participant)
- Bridges event attendance and post-event reflection (qualitative)

- **15 women** completing 60 hours of programming
- **15–25 children** receiving registered care
- **4 mentors** trained and matched
- **5–8 women** from a partner community joining the Bridges event
- **1 venue** activated as a women's community space every Saturday for 3 months
