## 2. The Project

**"The Welcome Table"** — a festive holiday-season dinner where newcomer families, guided by
Sahan volunteers, cook and serve a **halal dinner for ~70 Calgary seniors** who would otherwise
spend the season alone. Newcomer youth serve tables; every place setting has a small gift and a
printed welcome card connecting guests to community resources (211, library, settlement agencies,
Sahan). Pay-it-forward tickets ($10 subsidized / $25 community) — nobody turned away.

**Board approval:** Resolution passed **5/5** — board is pro-integration with Canadian values.

**The story in one line:** Newcomers give back to their new country in its season of generosity —
integration through contribution, not just adaptation.
