## 6. Mission, purpose & programs

- **Mission / purpose:** Sahan Heritage Foundation connects newcomers to essential services, equips newcomer families to navigate Canadian systems and institutions, and nurtures children through tutoring and social interaction — working toward a thriving, fully integrated newcomer community in Calgary. *(Draft wording used on grant applications — confirm against Sahan's own incorporation documents / constitution before treating as official, per the earlier checklist flag.)*
- **People served:** approximately **1,500 people per year** (across all events and programs).
- **Programs / services on file:** Connections (business communication, MS Office training, English tutoring, Conversation Circles); LINC English classes; New Knowledge / New Steps (one-week newcomer orientation); Women's Program; and seasonal community events (e.g., BBQ, Stampede prep, an elders cohort).
- **Flagship project (in progress):** *Rise Together: Pathways for Newcomer Women* — employment-readiness, English-language, and community-belonging program (Calgary), applying to Alberta's Community Initiatives Program (CIP) Project Grant via the GATE Front Office portal.
